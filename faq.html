Questions, answered.

Everything about fees, batches and refunds in one place.

When does my batch start?

Your batch starts once 10 students have joined your chosen timing. We inform you by email and WhatsApp the moment it is ready.

What happens to the ₹400 seat fee?

It is adjusted in your total course fee of ₹3,999. After the demo class, you pay the remaining ₹3,599.

What if the batch is cancelled?

You receive a refund as soon as it is cancelled. ₹100 is deducted for the DOC B1 PDF course, so ₹300 is returned.

Can I change my timing later?

Once a batch is fixed, the timing cannot be changed directly. Send a request by email to ishwar@cowartu.com.

Which language are the classes in?

Classes are taught in Hindi.

Your seat is waiting.

Only 10 seats per batch. Book yours for ₹400 and start learning from the first Sunday.
