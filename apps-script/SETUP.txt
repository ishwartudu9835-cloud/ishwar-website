SETUP: save form data to Google Sheets (free)

1. Go to sheets.google.com and create a new blank sheet. Name it "Cowartu Admissions".
2. Click Extensions > Apps Script. Delete the sample code and paste everything from Code.gs. Click Save.
3. Click the gear icon (Project Settings). Under "Script properties" click Add script property.
   Name: ADMIN_KEY   Value: a strong password of your choice. This is your admin panel password.
4. Click Deploy > New deployment. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
   Execute as: Me
   Who has access: Anyone
   Click Deploy and allow the permissions. (If Google shows "unsafe", click Advanced > Go to project.)
5. Copy the Web app URL (it ends with /exec).
6. Open assets/js/config.js and paste it inside SHEET_URL:"" then upload the file to GitHub.
7. Open admin.html on your site, enter your password, and download the Excel file.

If you change Code.gs later, use Deploy > Manage deployments > Edit > New version > Deploy.
